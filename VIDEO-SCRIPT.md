# CiteDesk, 60 second demo video script

Target length: about 60 seconds of screen recording with narration.
Show the live demo page. Move at a steady pace. Total narration is
about 140 words, which reads in roughly one minute.

## Shot list and narration

**0 to 8s, the page and the pre-chat form.**
Show the CiteDesk header, fill the pre-chat form (name, product:
Doorbell Camera, topic: Order, shipping or returns), click Start chat.
Narration: "This is CiteDesk, a support bot with one rule: show the
proof, or hand off to a human."

**8 to 28s, a content-based answer, with proof.**
Type: "How long until my refund shows up in my account?" Send.
Pause on the answer, the citation chip, and the groundedness meter.
Narration: "Every answer is built only from the help articles. It shows
the article it came from and a groundedness score, so anyone can check
what the answer stands on."

**28 to 42s, the distinctive feature: escalation instead of guessing.**
Type: "Does NorthPeak sell solar panels or EV chargers?" Send.
Show the escalation message, then pan to the handoff brief panel.
Narration: "Ask something the knowledge base does not cover, and it
refuses to guess. The human agent gets a brief: the question, the
closest articles, why it escalated, and what they need."

**42 to 58s, one workflow, with an action.**
Click "Start a return". Choose: within 30 days, complete and
undamaged, original payment method. Land on the label step with the RMA
reference.
Narration: "Guided workflows cite the article behind each step. The
return flow checks the policy, then takes a real action and creates the
return label."

**58 to 60s, close.**
Narration: "CiteDesk. Proof with every answer, or a clean handoff."
